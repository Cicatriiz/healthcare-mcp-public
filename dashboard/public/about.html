About This Dashboard

Purpose

The FDA Drug Shortage Intelligence Dashboard provides healthcare providers, pharmacists, and hospital systems with real-time visibility into current drug shortages in the United States. By aggregating data from the FDA and enriching it with clinical context from PubMed, Health.gov, Clinical Trials, and other authoritative sources, this tool helps providers make informed decisions about patient care and supply chain management.

Data Sources

  • openFDA Drug Shortages API - Primary shortage data including status, manufacturers, reasons, and timelines
  • FDA Drug Labels - Detailed drug information, indications, warnings, and contraindications
  • PubMed - Peer-reviewed medical literature and research articles
  • Health.gov - Evidence-based health guidelines and patient information
  • ClinicalTrials.gov - Active and completed clinical trials
  • medRxiv - Pre-print medical research articles
  • NCBI Bookshelf - Biomedical literature and textbooks
  • NLM Medical Terminology - ICD-10 codes and medical terminology

Features

🔍 Advanced Search & Filtering

Search by drug name or NDC, filter by status, therapeutic class, dosage form, and manufacturer

📊 Analytics & Trends

Visualize shortage patterns by category, route, manufacturer, and geographic region

📚 Clinical Context

Automatic enrichment with research articles, guidelines, trials, and alternative therapies

🗺️ Geographic Distribution

Map shortages by manufacturer location to understand regional impacts

Data Refresh & Caching

  • Shortage Data: Cached for 6 hours (shortages update frequently)
  • Enrichment Data: Cached for 24 hours (relatively stable)
  • Analytics: Cached for 1 hour (computed from shortages)

Limitations

  • Geographic data is parsed from manufacturer contact information (MVP implementation)
  • Alternative drug suggestions are based on therapeutic category matching
  • Data is as current as the openFDA API (typically updated daily)
  • Some enrichment data may not be available for all drugs

Technology Stack

Backend: Node.js, Express, Model Context Protocol (MCP) tools
Frontend: HTML5, JavaScript (ES6+), Tailwind CSS
APIs: openFDA, PubMed E-utilities, ClinicalTrials.gov, Health.gov v4

Open source project: healthcare-mcp-public
