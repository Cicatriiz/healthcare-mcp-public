⚠️ Discontinued vs. Active Shortages

Important distinction: Some items in the FDA shortage database are marked as "discontinued" - these are products that manufacturers have permanently stopped producing, rather than temporary supply issues.

🔴 Active Shortage
Temporary supply disruption due to manufacturing, demand, regulatory, or raw material issues. Expected to be resolved.
⛔ Discontinued Product
Manufacturer has permanently ceased production. Alternative products or suppliers may be needed.
💡 Clinical Impact: Discontinued products require different mitigation strategies than temporary shortages - healthcare facilities should identify therapeutic alternatives rather than waiting for supply restoration.
